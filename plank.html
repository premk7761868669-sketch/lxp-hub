Plank: How Long Should You Hold It?

The plank builds the deep core muscles that protect your spine. Many people hold it for too long with poor form. Quality beats time.

How to do it

  1. Rest your forearms on the floor with elbows under your shoulders.
  2. Step your feet back so your body forms a straight line.
  3. Squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button in.
  4. Look at the floor, breathe normally and stop when your form breaks.

Common mistakes

  • Hips sagging toward the floor, which stresses the lower back.
  • Hips pointing up like a tent.
  • Holding your breath.

How to progress

Beginners can start with 20 seconds and add 10 seconds each level. For most people, three clean 30 to 60 second holds are better than one shaky two-minute hold. Once 60 seconds feels easy, make it harder with side planks or by lifting one leg.

About LXP Fit

LXP Fit is a free fitness tracker that counts your reps with your phone camera, so the numbers are honest. You level up by hitting your target on several days, earn rewards, and follow guides for good form.

It is built by Prem Kumar (known online as LXP GHOST), a video editor and content creator from India who wanted a workout app that actually checks your effort instead of trusting a button.

Our promise

Your camera video never leaves your phone and we do not create accounts. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. This policy explains how LXP Fit (the "site" and "app") handles information.

Camera

The rep counter uses your camera to detect body movement. Video is processed only inside your browser on your device. It is not recorded, uploaded or shared.

Data stored on your device

Your levels, reps, streaks and Premium status are saved in your browser's local storage. We do not have accounts or a database, and we cannot see this data. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs such as IP address. Pose-detection files are loaded from jsDelivr and Google Cloud Storage; no camera data is sent to them. Premium payments are made by UPI outside this site and we do not store payment details.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: see the Contact page.

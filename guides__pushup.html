How to Do a Push-Up Correctly

The push-up trains your chest, shoulders, triceps and core with no equipment. Done well, it is one of the best bodyweight exercises you can do anywhere.

How to do it

  1. Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder width, fingers pointing forward.
  2. Extend your legs so your body forms one straight line from head to heels.
  3. Tighten your stomach and glutes, then lower your chest toward the floor with elbows at about 45 degrees.
  4. Push the floor away until your arms are straight again.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the hips sag or pile up toward the ceiling.
  • Flaring the elbows straight out to the sides.
  • Doing half reps. Lower until your chest is a fist away from the floor.

How to progress

Start with wall or knee push-ups if full ones are too hard. Once you can do 10 clean reps, add a few reps every few days. Later you can try archer, one-hand and finger-tip variations, but only after your wrists and tendons are well conditioned.

How to Recover Between Workouts

Muscles get stronger while you rest, not while you train. Good recovery habits help you improve and lower the risk of injury.

Daily recovery habits

  1. Sleep around 7 to 9 hours when possible.
  2. Eat regular meals with enough protein and drink water through the day.
  3. Do a 5 minute warm-up before training and gentle stretching after.
  4. Take one or two lighter days a week, such as an easy walk.

Common mistakes

  • Training the same muscles hard every single day.
  • Skipping sleep to fit in more exercise.
  • Ignoring sharp or lasting pain.

How to know you can push harder

You can push harder when you wake up feeling mostly fresh and your last session felt manageable. Increase the work in small steps, for example a few more reps, and keep weekly consistency over perfect days. See a doctor for pain that does not go away.

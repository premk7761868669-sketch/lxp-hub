Lunges: Build Strong Legs and Balance

Lunges train each leg separately, which improves balance and fixes strength differences between your left and right sides.

How to do it

  1. Stand tall and take a big step forward.
  2. Lower your back knee toward the floor until both knees are bent near 90 degrees.
  3. Keep your front knee over your ankle, not far past your toes.
  4. Push through the front heel to return, then switch legs.

Common mistakes

  • Taking too short a step, which loads the knee.
  • Leaning the torso far forward.
  • Letting the front knee cave inward.

How to progress

Start with 8 to 10 reps per leg. When that feels easy, slow the lowering phase, try walking lunges or hold a backpack with books. Rest a day between hard leg sessions.

Stop if you feel sharp pain and talk to a doctor if you have an injury or medical condition.

Burpees: A Full-Body Exercise Done Safely

The burpee combines a squat, a plank, a push-up and a jump. It raises your heart rate fast and works almost every muscle, so good form and rest matter.

How to do it

  1. Stand with feet shoulder width apart.
  2. Squat down and place your hands on the floor.
  3. Step or jump your feet back into a plank, body in a straight line.
  4. Optional: do a push-up, then bring your feet back toward your hands.
  5. Stand up and, if you want, finish with a small jump.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the hips sag in the plank position.
  • Rushing the reps until your form falls apart.
  • Landing hard on stiff knees.

How to progress

Begin with step-back burpees without a push-up or jump. Add the push-up first, the jump last. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets. This is intense, so check with a doctor first if you have heart, joint or breathing problems.
